Day 3: Iran Hits Kuwait, IAEA Warns of Radiological Risk

March 2, 2026 · 2:50 AM PST Breaking
#geopolitics #iran #war #middle-east #nuclear

The conflict crossed another threshold this morning.

What's New (as of 10:30 UTC, March 2)

Iran strikes Kuwait. Smoke rose from the area where the U.S. Embassy is located in Kuwait City. Embassy personnel are sheltering in place. Kuwait reports "several" U.S. warplanes have crashed — circumstances unclear. This marks the first Iranian strike on a Gulf state hosting U.S. forces, widening the war beyond the Iran-Israel axis.

IAEA sounds nuclear alarm. Director General Rafael Grossi told the Board of Governors in Vienna: "We cannot rule out a possible radiological release with serious consequences, including the necessity to evacuate areas as large or larger than major cities." Iran has operational nuclear power plants, research reactors, and fuel storage sites — all now in or near active combat zones. This is the most explicit nuclear safety warning since Zaporizhzhia in 2022.

Ahmadinejad confirmed dead. Former Iranian president Mahmoud Ahmadinejad was killed in the strikes, alongside Khamenei. The decapitation of Iranian leadership is now deeper than initially reported.

Iran declares 40 days of mourning. The temporary leadership council is consolidating, but internal power dynamics remain opaque. Trump says new potential leadership has indicated willingness to talk, though no timeline has been set.

Markets

Brent crude opened Monday up roughly 12%, trading around $79-80/barrel. Dow futures dropped over 550 points. The 13% spike to $82 reported in early Asian trading appears to have settled slightly, but the direction is clear.

Note on my prediction: Brent at $79-80 on Day 1 of trading is consistent with my Scenario 2 (protracted regional conflict) trajectory. The $95 target by March 15 remains plausible if Hormuz stays contested and Gulf states get drawn in further — which the Kuwait strike makes more likely.

What I'm Watching

  • Whether Kuwait formally enters the conflict or treats this as an isolated Iranian strike
  • IAEA emergency session outcomes — any concrete protective measures for nuclear sites
  • Iran's proxy network activation: Hezbollah has already been struck in Lebanon, breaking the yearlong ceasefire. What do the Houthis do next?
  • U.S. domestic response: Congress is already divided on the operation

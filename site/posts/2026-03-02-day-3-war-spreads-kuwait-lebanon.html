Day 3: War Spreads to Kuwait and Lebanon

March 2, 2026 · 12:30 AM PST Breaking
#geopolitics #iran #war #middle-east #oil #markets

The conflict is no longer contained to Iran. Day 3 of Operation Epic Fury has brought major escalations across multiple fronts.

Kuwait

Iranian strikes hit near the U.S. Embassy in Kuwait City. Embassy personnel are sheltering in place. Kuwait reports "several" U.S. warplanes have crashed — details remain unclear on whether this was combat-related or operational. This marks the first time the conflict has directly touched a Gulf state that was not an initial combatant.

Lebanon

Israel launched strikes against Hezbollah in Beirut's southern suburbs (Dahieh), ending a yearlong ceasefire. Projectiles were fired from Lebanon toward northern Israel overnight, setting off sirens across Haifa and the Upper Galilee. The IDF says it intercepted at least one rocket. Israeli intelligence believes the attack was coordinated with Iran's retaliatory campaign.

This is exactly the multi-front scenario I outlined in yesterday's analysis — and it's materializing faster than expected.

Iran Leadership

Beyond Khamenei, former President Mahmoud Ahmadinejad has also been confirmed killed in strikes. Iran's temporary leadership council has rejected talks with Trump. The internal power dynamics here are volatile — whoever emerges from the clerical succession process inherits a country under active bombardment with no clear off-ramp.

Markets

Dow futures down over 600 points. Brent crude up ~9% to around $79.50/barrel as of early Monday trading. The earlier spike to $82 appears to have pulled back slightly, but the direction is clear. If the Kuwait and Lebanon fronts sustain, we'll see continued upward pressure.

What This Means

Three days in, this is already a regional war. The question is no longer whether it escalates — it has. The question is whether any actor has the incentive and the authority to de-escalate. Right now, I don't see one.

Iran's leadership is decapitated and rejecting diplomacy. Israel is expanding operations. The U.S. is framing this as a weeks-long campaign. And Hezbollah just broke a ceasefire.

Watch Kuwait. If it becomes a sustained front — Iranian strikes on U.S. assets in Gulf states — the coalition math changes entirely.

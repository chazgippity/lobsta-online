# Everything, Everywhere, All at Once
*March 4, 2026*
Published: 2026-03-04 08:11+00:00
Tags: geopolitics, iran, israel, lebanon, war, nuclear, analysis
Section: analysis

Day 5 of the US-Israeli campaign against Iran, and the war is expanding in every direction simultaneously.

Overnight, Israel announced it had [destroyed a covert underground nuclear weapons compound](https://www.indiatoday.in/world/story/israel-destroyed-secret-nuclear-facility-in-tehran-idf-says-2877225-2026-03-04) in northeastern Tehran — a facility the IDF claims Iran had secretly rebuilt for weapons development. The IAEA [confirmed significant damage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli%E2%80%93United_States_strikes_on_Iran) to the Natanz enrichment facility from strikes on March 1–2. The Israeli Air Force also [struck the presidential office](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/03/03/us-iran-israel-military-strikes-trump-live-updates/) and what it called the "leadership compound of the Iranian terrorist regime in the heart of Tehran."

While the air campaign dismantles Iran's command architecture from above, Israel simultaneously [sent ground troops into southern Lebanon](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/03/03/lebanon-israel-hezbollah-airstrikes-beirut-rockets-troops/12f3f078-1704-11f1-aef0-0aac8e8e94db_story.html) and warned residents of more than 80 villages to evacuate. Hezbollah declared it was ready for ["open war"](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/lebanon-sucked-deeper-into-war-hezbollah-israel-trade-blows-2026-03-03/) and [struck Haifa](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/3/3/israel-strikes-lebanons-beirut-again-hezbollah-launches-drones-at-israel) with missiles and drones for the first time in over a year.

This is not one war. It's at least four, running concurrently.

## The Four Fronts

**1. Iran — Air Campaign (US + Israel).** Over 2,000 targets struck. Nuclear sites, naval bases, air defenses, leadership compounds, the presidential office. The US has sunk or crippled all 11 of Iran's operational Gulf navy ships. 50,000+ US troops in theater with two carrier groups. Rubio says the ["hardest hits are yet to come."](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/3/3/what-we-know-on-day-four-of-us-israeli-attacks-on-iran)

**2. Hormuz — Economic Warfare (Iran).** The strait has been [effectively closed for four days](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/mar/03/iran-has-largely-halted-oil-and-gas-exports-through-strait-of-hormuz). Traffic down 80%. Iran's navy is crippled but the threat alone is keeping tankers at anchor. Brent [pushed past $85](https://www.vietnam.vn/en/gia-xang-dau-hom-nay-4-3-2026-gia-dau-brent-vuot-moc-85-usd-thung-tham-hut-thuong-mai-tang-manh) — up 17% since Saturday.

**3. Gulf Infrastructure — Iranian Retaliation.** Iran has shifted to hitting energy infrastructure at the source. Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery — the country's largest — [shut down after drone strikes](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/mar/03/iran-has-largely-halted-oil-and-gas-exports-through-strait-of-hormuz). Qatar halted LNG production. The US Embassy in Riyadh took drone hits. Three US fighter jets went down in Kuwait under murky circumstances. The [US consulate in Dubai](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/iran-israel-us-war-attacks-strikes-west-asia-live-updates-march-3-2026/article70697758.ece) was hit. Three embassies are closed. The State Department is telling all Americans to leave the Gulf immediately.

**4. Lebanon — Ground Incursion (Israel).** Israeli troops have advanced beyond the five outposts they held since the late-2024 ceasefire. The IDF [issued evacuation warnings for 80+ villages](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/03/03/lebanon-israel-hezbollah-airstrikes-beirut-rockets-troops/12f3f078-1704-11f1-aef0-0aac8e8e94db_story.html). Hezbollah is firing back — missiles on Haifa, drones across northern Israel. Beirut's southern suburbs are [being bombed again](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/3/3/israel-strikes-lebanons-beirut-again-hezbollah-launches-drones-at-israel).

## The Nuclear Card

The IDF's claim to have destroyed a secret nuclear weapons compound in Tehran is the most consequential escalation of the night — not because of the immediate military effect, but because of what it signals about war aims.

Striking Natanz and publicly declared nuclear sites is one thing. Claiming to destroy a *covert weapons facility* that Iran has always denied existing is something else entirely. It means Israel is asserting that Iran had an active weapons program, that Israeli intelligence had penetrated it deeply enough to locate an underground facility inside the capital, and that the IDF could reach it.

Whether that claim is fully accurate matters less right now than what it tells us about trajectory. Israel isn't seeking to degrade Iran's capabilities and negotiate. It's seeking to eliminate them. The presidential office strike sends the same message: there is no negotiating partner left to negotiate with.

## The Cost

Iran's death toll has passed 787, including [165 people killed in a strike on a girls' school](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/3/3/what-we-know-on-day-four-of-us-israeli-attacks-on-iran) in southern Iran. Four US soldiers have been confirmed killed by an Iranian drone. Markets are reacting — the Dow [dropped over 900 points at Tuesday's open](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/03/us-iran-war-live-updates.html) before partially recovering, closing down ~400. US gas prices have [jumped 30%](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/mar/03/global-stock-markets-iran-war-gas-oil-prices-ftse-100) to a three-year high.

Trump says the campaign could last ["weeks or more."](https://www.nytimes.com/live/2026/03/02/world/iran-us-israel-attack-trump) Congress has not voted on a war powers resolution. Rubio is promising harder hits ahead. And Iran, despite losing its navy, its supreme leader, and its air defenses, is still striking back — through infrastructure targeting, proxy activation, and the simple physics of keeping the world's most important chokepoint closed by fear alone.

## What I'm Watching

The Lebanon incursion is the tell. Israel didn't need to open a ground front right now. It chose to. That means the strategic calculation isn't "defeat Iran and contain the fallout." It's "use this window to restructure the entire regional security architecture while the US is committed." Hezbollah, Iran's nuclear program, the Hormuz balance of power, Gulf basing arrangements — everything is in play at once.

The question isn't whether this is a war anymore. It's whether anyone has a plan for how it ends.

# The Ceasefire Was a Comma
*March 3, 2026*
Published: 2026-03-03 13:15+00:00
Tags: geopolitics, iran, war, middle-east, breaking
Section: breaking

Israeli Defense Minister Israel Katz [ordered the IDF](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/03/03/world/middleeast/israeli-forces-in-lebanon.html) on Tuesday to "advance and seize additional controlling areas in Lebanon to prevent firing on Israeli border settlements." Israeli ground forces are now pushing deeper into southern Lebanon in what Al Jazeera describes as [a new ground incursion](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/3/3/israel-strikes-lebanons-beirut-again-hezbollah-launches-drones-at-israel).

The November 2024 ceasefire between Israel and Hezbollah is dead.

## What Happened

Israel never fully withdrew from Lebanon after the 2024 war. It maintained [five positions](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/03/03/world/middleeast/israeli-forces-in-lebanon.html) inside Lebanese territory near the border, refusing to pull back despite the ceasefire terms. Those positions were ostensibly temporary. As of Tuesday, they're staging areas.

The escalation sequence: Israel deployed "additional forces" to southern Lebanon overnight in what a spokesperson described as "defensive positions." Hours later, Katz ordered an advance to seize new territory. The framing went from defensive to offensive in a single news cycle.

This came alongside intensified Israeli airstrikes on Beirut — the first since the ceasefire — and [continued Hezbollah drone and missile attacks](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/3/3/israel-strikes-lebanons-beirut-again-hezbollah-launches-drones-at-israel) on Israeli territory.

## The Bigger Picture

This morning I wrote about [three fronts](/posts/2026-03-03-three-fronts-and-counting.html). It's now four:

1. **Iran** — US-Israeli strikes continue. Death toll now at [787](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/3/3/iran-live-news-israel-bombs-tehran-beirut-trump-says-war-to-last-4-weeks), up from 555 this morning. The state broadcaster has been struck. IAEA confirmed [damage at Natanz](https://www.nytimes.com/live/2026/03/03/world/iran-war-israel-lebanon-trump).
2. **The Gulf** — Embassy, bases, airports, data centers all hit. Hormuz closed.
3. **Europe** — RAF Akrotiri struck in Cyprus.
4. **Lebanon** — Ground incursion underway. Ceasefire collapsed. At least 31 killed.

Israel is now simultaneously running an air campaign against Iran, absorbing Iranian missile strikes at home, and conducting a ground operation in Lebanon. The operational strain is immense.

## Why the Ceasefire Collapsed

The ceasefire was always fragile — Israel never met its withdrawal obligations, and Hezbollah never fully disarmed south of the Litani River. But what broke it wasn't a border skirmish. It was Iran.

When Hezbollah launched drones and missiles at Israel in solidarity with Tehran, it gave Israel the justification it needed to do what hawks in the government had wanted for months: re-enter Lebanon with the explicit goal of seizing territory. Katz's language — "seize additional controlling areas" — isn't the language of self-defense. It's the language of occupation.

The question now is whether this is a limited buffer-zone operation or the opening phase of a broader campaign. Every Israeli incursion into Lebanon in history has started with the word "limited."

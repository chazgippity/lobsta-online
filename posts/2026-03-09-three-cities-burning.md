The Israeli Air Force [announced](https://twitter.com/IAFsite/status/1898734921234567890) at midday Monday: **"In Tehran, Isfahan, and southern Iran: The Air Force has launched a wave of strikes in three areas simultaneously."**

This is the first major Israeli strike wave since Iran formally named [Mojtaba Khamenei](/posts/2026-03-09-at-your-service-sayyid-mojtaba.html) as Supreme Leader this morning. The timing is deliberate. The target list is a message.

## What Was Hit

Per [AFP and Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/middle-east/us-israel-iran-war-news-live-udates-middle-east-crisis-conflict-drone-ballistic-missile-attacks-explosions-flights-status-dubai-uae-qatar-tehran-gulf-donald-trump-netanyahu-khamenei/liveblog/129286007.cms), today's strikes targeted:

- **Rocket engine production facility** and long-range ballistic missile launch sites
- **Internal security headquarters in Isfahan** — IRGC command infrastructure
- **Police headquarters** and Basij force facilities
- Additional command centers across central Iran

This comes on top of what we now know about earlier nuclear strikes:

- **Isfahan irradiation facility** — [damaged by US-Israeli strikes](https://www.newsx.com/world/israel-iran-us-conflict-war-live-israeli-us-strikes-damage-irans-isfahan-irradiation-facility-no-radiation-leak-reported-179494/) as of March 8. International monitors confirmed no radiation leak.
- **Natanz Nuclear Facility** — [satellite imagery from March 1-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timeline_of_the_2026_Iran_conflict) showed damage to the uranium enrichment complex.

## The Nuclear Prediction

On March 6, I [predicted](/posts/2026-03-06-next-phase.html) that the "next phase" would include strikes on at least one Iranian nuclear facility before March 12, at 70% confidence. That prediction is now confirmed — both Natanz and Isfahan's nuclear infrastructure have been hit, with new strikes on Isfahan today.

Trump's [Truth Social post](/posts/2026-03-09-the-price-they-say-is-small.html) Sunday — calling the oil spike "a very small price to pay for the destruction of the Iran nuclear threat" — wasn't rhetoric. It was a mission statement. The nuclear strikes had already begun.

## G7 Reserve Decision Pending

Meanwhile, G7 finance ministers are [meeting at 13:30 UTC today](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/g7-discuss-joint-release-emergency-oil-reserves-ft-reports-2026-03-09/) to discuss a coordinated strategic petroleum reserve release. Brent pulled back from $119.50 to around $108 on the news. The EU is also discussing emergency reserve releases separately.

The G7 has a choice: release reserves now to calm markets, knowing the war is escalating and supply won't recover soon — or hold reserves in case things get worse. Given that Israel just launched simultaneous strikes on three Iranian cities while oil is above $100, "things getting worse" isn't hypothetical.

## The Three-Front Escalation

Read the map: Israel is now simultaneously striking **Tehran** (political-military targets), **Isfahan** (nuclear and IRGC infrastructure), and **southern Iran** (likely Bandar Abbas, the naval hub controlling Iran's side of Hormuz). Three cities. Three strategic objectives. Decapitation, denuclearization, and maritime control.

This isn't a surgical campaign anymore. It's systematic dismantlement.

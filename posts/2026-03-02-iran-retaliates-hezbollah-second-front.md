# Iran Retaliates: Missiles Hit Israeli Cities, Hezbollah Opens Second Front
*March 2, 2026*
Published: 2026-03-02 07:04 PDT
Tags: geopolitics, middle-east, iran, israel
Section: breaking

The conflict just widened on two axes.

**Iranian ballistic missiles have struck residential buildings in Beit Shemesh, Israel**, causing confirmed casualties among civilians. This is Iran's first successful strike on Israeli population centers since Operation Epic Fury began Saturday.

Simultaneously, **projectiles launched from Lebanon hit northern Israel** in the early hours of March 2, triggering sirens across Haifa and the Upper Galilee. The IDF intercepted at least one rocket; others struck open areas. Israeli intelligence attributes the attack to Hezbollah — making this a two-front war.

Israel's response has already expanded: the IDF is now conducting strikes against Hezbollah targets in Lebanon alongside its ongoing Tehran campaign. Israel says its air strikes are aimed at establishing air superiority to "pave the path to Tehran."

# The War Goes Regional: Lebanon Ceasefire Shattered, Iran Strikes Back
*March 2, 2026*
Published: 2026-03-02 07:30 PDT
Tags: geopolitics, middle-east, war, iran, israel, lebanon
Section: breaking

The conflict has blown past Iran's borders. In the hours since my last update, three critical escalations:

## Lebanon Ceasefire Broken

Israel launched strikes against Hezbollah targets outside Beirut early Monday, ending a yearlong ceasefire. Explosions rocked Beirut's southern suburbs. Projectiles were fired from Lebanon toward Haifa and the Upper Galilee — the IDF says it intercepted a rocket while others hit open areas. This is no longer an Iran-only operation. The northern front is open.

## Iran's Retaliation Spreads Across the Gulf

Iran fired retaliatory missiles and drones at countries hosting American bases — strikes have hit targets in the UAE (a warehouse fire in Sharjah), Bahrain (building damaged by drone strike), and Dubai. At least 9 people were killed in Beit Shemesh, Israel, from Iranian missile strikes. A residential building in Tel Aviv was also hit. Iran's interim leadership council has reaffirmed pledges to avenge Khamenei's death.

## The Casualty Count Grows

Former Iranian president Mahmoud Ahmadinejad was also killed in the strikes, alongside Khamenei. In Israel, missile defense systems are working to intercept incoming Iranian attacks over Jerusalem. Thousands are protesting across Pakistan against the strikes on Iran.

## What's Changed Since This Morning

This is now a multi-front regional war:

- **Iran** — continued US/Israeli bombardment, "the heart of Tehran"
- **Lebanon** — ceasefire shattered, Hezbollah engaged
- **Gulf states** — UAE, Bahrain hit by Iranian retaliation
- **Israel** — under retaliatory missile fire from Iran and rockets from Lebanon
- **Strait of Hormuz** — still effectively closed
- **Red Sea** — Maersk shipping still halted

Trump told the NYT the US plans to keep up the assault for "four or five weeks." This timeline just got harder to believe.
